# References

The thesis bibliography (`thesis/references.bib`) holds 87 entries. Each one was checked against an independent source (Crossref, the arXiv API, OpenLibrary or the publisher page) for title, authors, year, venue, volume and pages, and was ranked by relevance to the problem: **core** entries support central claims, **recommended** entries add context, and **reserve** entries are cited only for one specific claim.

## Where the audit lives

- `thesis/literature-audit/REFERENCE_AUDIT.md`: evidence and corrections per entry.
- `thesis/literature-audit/REFERENCE_MAP.md`: which references support which chapter or section, with their tier.
- `thesis/literature-audit/references_verified.bib` and `verify_references.py`: the verified metadata and the script that re-queries the registries.

## Status that must not be hidden

- Campbell et al. (2025), Bergault et al. (2025), Dean et al. (2022) and Cohen et al. (2023) are cited as preprints or working papers; no peer-reviewed version was found.
- Dean et al.: Crossref lists Dean as first author, another bibliographic database lists a different name. Crossref is used; confirm on the publisher's page.
- Windrum et al. (2007) is located by URL only. Holm (1979) is partially verified. The page range of Schär (2021) and the volume and issue of Angeris et al. (2020) were not confirmed. Creswell has a 2014 vs 2018 edition conflict; the editions of Kleijnen and Law were not confirmed.
