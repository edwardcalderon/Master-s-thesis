## Research question and hypothesis

**Question:** Under which combinations of arbitrage activity, liquidity conditions and heterogeneous behavior is hybrid CEX/DEX tracking stable or fragile? How much of the observed behavior of a stochastic agent-based model (ABM) can be explained by a tractable reduced nonlinear dynamical model?

**H1:** Stability depends on the balance between corrective arbitrage feedback and potentially amplifying behavioral/liquidity feedback.

- **H1a:** Constrained but sufficiently effective arbitrage reduces discrepancies after isolated shocks under stated execution assumptions.
- **H1b:** Proportional liquidity withdrawal increases the impact of subsequent fixed-size swaps, and adaptive LP rules may amplify shocks relative to static-liquidity counterfactuals.
- **H1c:** Local analytical stability conditions partially classify ABM simulation regimes; their failure boundaries are also scientific results.

## Formal definitions

- `e_t = log(p_dex,t / p_cex,t)` tracking error; `D_t = max(abs(e_t)-gamma_t, 0)` excess outside cost-dependent no-arbitrage band.
- Fee-free constant-product swap: `x*y=k`, marginal quote `p=y/x`, post-sell price `p' = p/(1+q/x)^2`.
- Discrete stochastic ABM: `S(t+1)=F(S(t), xi(t), J(t); theta)`.
- Reduced candidate: `e_dot=-a*tanh(e)+b*tanh(z)+u; z_dot=c*tanh(e)-d*z`; for `a,d>0; b,c>=0`, local asymptotic stability at origin if `ad>bc`; instability if `ad<bc`; inconclusive at equality. **This is a proposed surrogate, not a validated market law.**
- Arbitrage-band illustration: `e_dot=-a*sign(e)*max(abs(e)-gamma,0)+u`; Lyapunov function `V=0.5*D^2` and bounded-input bound hold under simplified assumptions.

## Bounded research scope

**In:** exogenous CEX price, v2-compatible constant-product AMM, four agent categories (noise, trend, arbitrage, adaptive LP), E0-E3, reduced-model Lyapunov/Jacobian/perturbation analysis, controlled Monte Carlo, verification, empirical mechanism comparison.

**Out:** profitability/prediction, bidirectional CEX/DEX contagion when CEX is exogenous, full order-book implementation, v3/v4 concentrated liquidity equivalence, real-money execution, bridges, lending, complex MEV, full network simulation.

**Scientific standard:** Mathematical proof of a reduced ODE is **not** proof of stochastic ABM stability. Report where theory/simulation disagree.
