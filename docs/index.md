# Research brief

This is the public entry point for the master thesis. The project is intentionally organized as a research workspace rather than a software product: the question comes first, the evidence stays inspectable, and the final PDF remains connected to its source.

## What is here?

- `apps/web/` is the public landing page and artifact viewer.
- `notebooks/` contains small, linkable Jupyter notebooks.
- `thesis/` contains the LaTeX source for the written argument.
- `data/` marks where raw and processed datasets belong without committing private or heavyweight files.

The research question, population, variables, and final contribution should be added here as the thesis stabilizes. Until then, the scaffold keeps the public architecture honest without inventing findings.
