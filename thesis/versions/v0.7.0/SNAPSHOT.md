# Manuscript source snapshot v0.7.0

Prepared November 25, 2026. This bilingual revision adds a bounded quadratic Lyapunov
analysis for the condition-specific fitted affine maps and clarifies that the recorded
accounting vector is not asserted to be a Markov-complete state. It reports numerical
solutions of the discrete Lyapunov equation using the exploration fits. The certificates
apply only to zero-input autonomous reduced maps; they do not establish stability of
the nonlinear, piecewise, stochastic simulator. The experiment, research findings, and
their interpretation remain unchanged from v0.6.1.

The snapshot includes both LaTeX entry points and chapters, bibliography, confirmatory
exports, and supplementary engineering exports. The workspace release version is
recorded independently in each LaTeX entry point. Earlier snapshots remain immutable.
