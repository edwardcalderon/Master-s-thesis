\chapter{Objectives}
\label{cap:objetivos}

\section{General objective}

To distinguish the mechanical consequences of a zero-fee constant-product AMM from the effects of specified agent rules, and to characterize bounded-arbitrage tracking, liquidity-withdrawal sensitivity and reduced-model performance within a reproducible synthetic CEX/DEX micro-world.

\section{Specific objectives}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{OE\arabic*.}, leftmargin=3em]
  \item To specify and verify a discrete simulator of a constant-product AMM with an exogenous CEX reference, noise, trend, arbitrage and liquidity-provision rules, and to record its runs reproducibly.
  \item To derive the ideal correction rate implied by the arbitrage capacity and compare it with finite-horizon simulator trajectories under the configured order flows.
  \item To characterize how the existing one-time withdrawal trigger changes simulated tracking under fixed-size and reserve-proportional orders, including the panic-agent ablation, without treating the rule as empirically estimated provider behavior.
  \item To assess the descriptive accuracy of reduced maps on held-out simulator tracking paths against persistence and a scalar AR(1), compare band-aware recursive replay with an affine map, and delimit predictive claims from regime classification.
\end{enumerate}
