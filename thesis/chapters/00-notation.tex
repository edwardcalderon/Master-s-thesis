\chapter*{Notation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Notation}

The following symbols are used throughout the document. The superscript $\mathrm{D}$ denotes the decentralized exchange (AMM) and $\mathrm{C}$ the centralized reference.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X@{}}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning \\
\midrule
$x_t,\ y_t$ & AMM reserves of assets $X$ and $Y$ at tick $t$ (price quoted in units of $Y$ per unit of $X$). \\
$k_t = x_t y_t$ & Reserve product; constant in a zero-fee swap. \\
$\pD_t = y_t/x_t$ & AMM marginal price. \\
$\pC_t$ & Centralized reference price (exogenous). \\
$q$ & Amount of $X$ sold to the AMM in a swap. \\
$f$ & Proportional fee retained by the pool. \\
$I(q,x)$ & Logarithmic impact of a sale: $2\log(1+q/x)$ without fee. \\
$e_t = \log(\pD_t/\pC_t)$ & Logarithmic tracking error. \\
$\gamma$ & Logarithmic half-width of the operating no-arbitrage band (executed value: $0.003$). \\
$d_t,\ D_t = |d_t|$ & Excess error outside the band with sign, and its absolute value. \\
$c$ & Arbitrage capacity: maximum fraction of the tradable reserve per tick. \\
$q^{\ast}$ & Sale size that takes the price to the edge of the band. \\
$z_t$ & Net buying pressure (noise and trend; no arbitrage) normalized by the initial reserve of $Y$. \\
$u_t = -\Delta\log \pC_t$ & Exogenous input of the reduced map. \\
$\alpha,\ \beta,\ \chi,\ \rho$ & Coefficients of the two-state map; $a = 1-\alpha$ and $c_z = -\chi$ in the fit tables. \\
$A$ & Local matrix of the autonomous map; $\operatorname{tr}A$ and $\det A$ its trace and determinant. \\
$H$ & Horizon of the response window (20 ticks). \\
$A_{\mathrm{ex}} = \sum_{t=1}^{H} D_t$ & Excess-error area (primary metric of H1). \\
$M = \max_{1\le t\le H}\bigl|\log(\pD_t/\pD_0)\bigr|$ & Maximum response of the AMM price (primary metric of H2). \\
$\Delta_1,\Delta_2,\Delta_3$ & Paired contrasts of H1, H2 and H3. \\
$\mathrm{MAE}$ & Mean absolute one-step prediction error (primary metric of H3). \\
E0--E3 & Experimental scenarios (table~\ref{tab:escenarios}). \\
OE1--OE4 & Specific objectives. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
