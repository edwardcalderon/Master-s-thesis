\section{Market model and observables}

Let $p_t^C>0$ denote the external CEX price and $p_t^D>0$ the marginal AMM price.
In a constant-product pool without swap fees, $x_ty_t=k_t$ and $p_t^D=y_t/x_t$. The
logarithmic tracking error is

\begin{equation}
  e_t=\log\left(\frac{p_t^D}{p_t^C}\right), \qquad
  D_t=(|e_t|-\gamma_t)_+,
\end{equation}

where $\gamma_t\geq 0$ is an operational no-arbitrage band whose costs, direction, and
dependence on trade size must be justified. The set
$E_\gamma=\{e:|e|\leq\gamma\}$ is not the same as the point equilibrium $e=0$. For a
sale of $q>0$ units of asset $x$ in an ideal AMM without fees:

\begin{equation}
  p^{D\prime}=\frac{p^D}{(1+q/x)^2}, \qquad
  \Delta\log p^D=-2\log(1+q/x).
\end{equation}

If an LP withdraws a proportion of the reserves, leaving $rx$ and $ry$ for $0<r<1$,
the immediate marginal price does not change; the impact of the same sale does
increase. Rules for LP additions and withdrawals and for fees distinguish
transactions that preserve $xy=k$ from those that change $k$.

\subsection{Full model}

\begin{equation}
  S_{t+1}=F(S_t,\xi_t,J_t;\theta),
\end{equation}

where the state includes prices, reserves, inventories, accounts, policy memory, and
liquidity variables; $\xi_t$ denotes random innovations, $J_t$ experimental shocks,
and $\theta$ the parameters. Primary metrics include excess error, sustained recovery
time, maximum response, depth, slippage, cascade frequency, and sensitivity to random
seeds. The CEX remains exogenous; the model will not claim bidirectional contagion.

\section{A traceable reduced map and local criteria}

\subsection{Why the initial ODE is replaced}

The earlier draft proposed an ODE with hyperbolic functions and Lyapunov/ISS
arguments before a simulator existed to connect its states and coefficients to swaps,
arbitrage capacity, or LP rules. That formulation is premature: the ABM runs in
discrete ticks and events, and an ODE is not valid simply because it is mathematically
convenient \cite{roubal2010linearization}. This version replaces it as the manuscript's
core with a discrete map whose semantics match the simulator's update order.

\subsection{Discrete price-tracking map}

Define signed excess relative to the no-arbitrage band as

\begin{equation}
  d_t=\operatorname{sgn}(e_t)(|e_t|-\gamma)_+.
\end{equation}

Let $z_t$ be net buying pressure in the DEX, positive when it raises $p_t^D$, and let
$u_t$ be the displacement induced by the CEX reference. A candidate local
approximation, to be tested against ABM data rather than imposed on it, is

\begin{align}
  d_{t+1}&=(1-\alpha)d_t+\beta z_t+u_t+\varepsilon_t,\\
  z_{t+1}&=-\chi d_t+\rho z_t+\eta_t.
\end{align}

Here $\alpha\geq0$ represents effective arbitrage correction, $\beta>0$ the local
impact of buying pressure, $\chi\geq0$ corrective feedback toward the band, and
$\rho$ the persistence of pressure. These are parameters of a local approximation,
not universal market constants.

Without inputs or noise, the linearization is

\begin{equation}
  \begin{pmatrix} d_{t+1}\\ z_{t+1} \end{pmatrix}
  =A\begin{pmatrix} d_t\\ z_t \end{pmatrix},\qquad
  A=\begin{pmatrix}1-\alpha&\beta\\-\chi&\rho\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}

For this $2\times2$ map, local Schur stability requires the roots of $A$ to lie
inside the unit circle; equivalently,

\begin{equation}
  1-\det(A)>0,\qquad
  1-\operatorname{tr}(A)+\det(A)>0,\qquad
  1+\operatorname{tr}(A)+\det(A)>0.
\end{equation}

These inequalities are diagnostic criteria for the reduced map. They do not prove
stability of an ABM with noise, saturation, capital limits, liquidity changes, or
additional agents.

\subsection{Operating regimes and a bounded use of multiple models}

Medina et al. \cite{medina2009multimodelos} describe nonlinear dynamics using local
linear models associated with operating points. This thesis does not adopt their fault
diagnosis component or attempt to identify a real financial system. The reference is
used narrowly to document CEX scenarios with explicit operating regions---normal
reference, shock, and recovery---each with declared perturbation and execution
parameters. This separation supports sensitivity analysis without claiming that the
regimes are empirical estimates or adding a new agent class.

\subsection{Exact mechanics anchoring the map}

The discrete form is linked to the AMM by a verifiable identity. If the DEX receives
a sale of $q>0$ units of $x$, then
$\Delta\log p^D=-2\log(1+q/x)$. An arbitrageur acting when $|e|>\gamma$ attempts to
bring the price back to the band, but its tradable reserve fraction is capacity
limited. Therefore, $\alpha$ is not postulated as an abstract gain: it is estimated or
diagnosed from the observed contraction of excess $d_t$ per tick. Liquidity enters
directly because the same absolute sale increases $q/x$ when an LP withdraws reserves.
